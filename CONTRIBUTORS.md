## How to contribute

Code, documentation, bug reports, testing, accessibility feedback, and design improvements all help. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.

Use your preferred author name in commits. Contributors may add themselves to this file in a pull request, with a short description of their actual contribution. A public profile link is optional; personal contact details are not required. Do not add another person's identity without their consent.
